**Description (4000)**

```
Ban Khoi is a 3D game for children aged 4 to 7, including children who cannot read yet. All guidance is given by voice and pictures.

Your child steers a cute block buddy around a huge island made of cubes. Glowing beams mark quests. At each one, one tap makes the buddy run to fetch every wooden block, carry it over its head and stack it on the wall to form a number or a letter. Each block that lands is named out loud.

WHAT CHILDREN LEARN
- The digits 0 to 9
- All 29 letters of the Vietnamese alphabet, including accented ones
- Counting blocks one by one
- Building short Vietnamese words from letter cards: father, mother, grandma, grandpa, brother, sister, banana, pineapple, fish, cat, chicken, rabbit
- A first taste of gardening, cooking and caring for pets

WHAT IS ON THE ISLAND
- 40+ quests for numbers, letters and words
- A collectible card for every number and letter you build
- A block city with streets, tall buildings and a fountain
- An amusement park with a giant wheel and a carousel
- Car races and animal races: tap fast to win a golden block
- A farm: grow carrots, tomatoes and cabbages, harvest and cook
- Catch rabbits, chicks, pigs and sheep and keep them as pets
- Surprise gifts for your buddy: hats, crowns, glasses, bows, scarves, balloons
- Rivers, lakes, bridges, forests and hills
- Six block buddies to choose from

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
- No ads
- No in-app purchases
- No accounts and no data collection
- No links out of the app
- Works without an internet connection
```
